# QuizStrike game rules

QuizStrike is a team game that helps you practice English. You answer
questions while moving around an arena with your classmates.

Your teacher chooses the map, the time, and the game type. The game may have
several rounds.

## Rules for every game

- Be kind and use a school-safe nickname.
- Answer questions to earn rewards.
- Use rewards to buy more snowballs or special gear when the shop is open.
- A correct answer may also give you a special boost, depending on your
  teacher's settings.
- Snowballs are only part of the game. They do not cause real harm.
- Do not leave your team waiting. Help your teammates and keep moving.

Warmth is your game health. Snowballs lower your warmth. When your warmth
reaches zero, you are out for a while.

When the game asks you to interact with something, this usually means pressing
the **E** key. Your screen will show the right key to use.

If you answer incorrectly, your teacher may take away some rewards. Your
teacher chooses whether this rule is turned on.

## Capture the Flag

### The goal

The Red Team tries to take the flag to the Blue Team's base. The Blue Team
tries to stop them and capture the flag after Red places it.

### How to play

1. A Red player walks close to the flag and presses the interact key.
2. The Red player carries the flag to the Blue base.
3. The Red player presses the interact key again to place the flag.
4. Red protects the flag while the timer counts down.
5. Blue can walk close to the placed flag and press the interact key to capture
   it.

### How to win

- Red wins if the flag stays safe until the timer reaches zero.
- Blue wins if Blue captures the placed flag.
- Blue wins if the time runs out before Red places the flag.
- Red wins if every Blue player is out.
- Blue wins if every Red player is out before Red places the flag.

In this game, players who are out cannot return by answering practice
questions. Watch the game and cheer for your team.

## Team Tag

### The goal

Red and Blue try to tag players on the other team with snowballs. The team
with the most tags does best.

### How to play

1. Answer a question.
2. Move around the arena and look for players on the other team.
3. Throw snowballs when you are close enough.
4. Stay near your teammates so you can help each other.

### When you are out

If enough snowballs hit you, you are out for a while. If your teacher allows
practice answers, you can answer questions while you wait. Answer three
questions correctly to return with full warmth and fresh snowballs.

### How to win

- The team with more tags wins the round.
- If the teams have the same number of tags, the game checks which team had
  more players return to the game.
- If it is still even, the team with more quiz rewards wins the round.
- After all rounds, the team that won more rounds wins the game.

## Zombie Survival

### The goal

The Zombies try to turn every Human into a Zombie. The Humans try to keep at
least one Human safe until time runs out.

### How to play

1. At the start, the game secretly chooses some players to become Zombies.
2. Zombies are on the Red side. Humans are on the Blue side.
3. Humans answer questions to get running energy. A correct answer gives back
   some energy.
4. Humans use their energy to run away and stay safe.
5. Zombies chase Humans and can use snowballs to catch them.
6. When a Human is caught, that player becomes a Zombie and joins the Red side.

Humans cannot throw snowballs in Zombie Survival. Their best tools are good
answers, quick thinking, and teamwork.

### How to win

- Zombies win if every Human becomes a Zombie before time runs out.
- Humans win if at least one Human is still safe when time runs out.

Once a Human becomes a Zombie, they stay a Zombie for the rest of the game.

## A good game plan

- Answer questions carefully and quickly.
- Tell your teammates what you see.
- Save snowballs for a useful moment.
- Stay close to your team's important places.
- In Capture the Flag, protect the flag after Red places it.
- In Team Tag, help teammates who are being chased.
- In Zombie Survival, Humans should spread out and keep some running energy.
- In every game, learning is more important than winning.
