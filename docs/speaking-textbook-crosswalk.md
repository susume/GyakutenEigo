# R7 NEW HORIZON: School English speaking-test cross-reference

All 31 School English core tasks have an adapted example dialogue, relevant language additions, a communicative goal extension, and textbook references. Existing class language and visual contexts are retained. The examples are practice support, not compulsory scripts or an alternative source of route, menu, price or other task facts.

Sources reviewed: the supplied `r7nh_1_FULL.pdf`, `r7nh_2_FULL.pdf`, `r7nh_3_FULL.pdf`, and `R7_NH_単語・連語データベース_v1.xlsx` (Word List, topic words and Word List extra words). Connections include direct functional matches and adaptations to another situation or from writing to speaking. They are not claims that each new dialogue appears verbatim in the book.

Book pages are printed page numbers. PDF positions are one-based spread indices in the supplied files; these PDFs often contain two printed pages per spread. Vocabulary references identify the chosen sense in the workbook, which can differ from the word's earliest appearance. For example, change, take and stop use the transport senses from Book 2, page 77; may uses the permission sense, not the month May.
