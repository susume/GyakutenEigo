# QuizStrike VFX audit — 2026-08-16

## Outcome

The VFX pass adds a semantic, pooled runtime without adding a particle
dependency. The shipped external art is a seven-sprite subset of Kenney's
Particle Pack 1.1, licensed CC0. The selected runtime folder is 407,123 bytes
including its preserved license file.

Brackeys' VFX Bundle and Kronbits' 1000 Free Game Assets pack were checked as
CC0 alternatives but were not copied into the app because the Kenney subset
covers the current QuizStrike vocabulary with a smaller payload. Per-file
provenance is recorded in `apps/web/public/assets/vfx/LICENSES.md`.

## Runtime budget

| Quality | Active effects | Sprite capacity | Remote view range |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Performance / Low | 6 | 6 | 120 world units |
| Balanced / Medium | 12 | 24 | 200 world units |
| High | 16 | 48 | 280 world units |

Local feedback bypasses distance culling and receives admission priority.
Major objective and round cues can replace lower-priority ambient cues. Shared
geometry and fixed slot/material pools avoid per-event geometry allocation;
effects are recycled and fully disposed with the arena scene.

## Follow-up audit corrections

A second implementation audit fixed issues found after the initial pass:

- Hit confirmation now uses a short-lived independent marker. It no longer
  stays yellow after the first hit or replays when a later shot misses.
- Sprite rotation is applied through `SpriteMaterial.rotation`, which is the
  rotation consumed by Three.js's billboard shader.
- Pool admission searches all free slots before evicting an active effect,
  chooses the lowest-priority, oldest eligible effect when full, and never
  lets lower-priority ambience truncate a major cue.
- Reported sprite budgets now match the actual fixed capacities: 6/24/48.
- Snow, water, metal, stone, sand, dirt, and grass cues receive appropriate
  texture routing and neutral surface colors instead of defaulting to team
  blue.
- Heavy fire uses one correctly styled pooled world cue, and remote observers
  receive reduced-intensity snow/player/shield impacts without duplicating the
  attacker or target's local feedback.
- Local objective interactions and Blue/Red round results receive local
  admission priority and explicit victory/defeat treatment.
